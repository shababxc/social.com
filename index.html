❓ Help & FAQ

1. What is ToBOOSTAi? ▼
ToBOOSTAi is a Web3-powered social media boost and earning mini-app on Telegram. Users can earn points and $GRAM by completing tasks or spend them to boost their own channels and communities.
2. How can I earn points & $GRAM? ▼
You can earn rewards by:
• Completing Bounty Tasks (+200 Pts each)
• Watching Sponsored Video Ads (+50,000 Pts)
• Claiming Daily Check-in (+100 Pts)
• Inviting friends with our multi-tier referral program!
3. How can I use my points? ▼
Formula: 10,000 Points = 1 $GRAM. You can use your balance to publish exposure tasks for your own Telegram channels, groups, and social profiles.
4. How to boost a channel or post tasks? ▼
Click the "POST +" button on the home screen. Enter your task title and link, select desired impressions on the slider, and pay using Points, $GRAM via TON Connect, or Telegram Stars!
5. How does the daily task limit work? ▼
By default, every user can complete up to 5 bounty tasks per day. You can increase this limit up to 20 tasks per day by inviting friends (3 invites = 10 tasks, 6 invites = 15 tasks, 9 invites = 20 tasks).
